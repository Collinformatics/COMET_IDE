COmprehensive Motif Evaluation Toolkit

Process DNA

Extract and translate substrate DNA found between two flanking sequences on the 5' and 3' sides.
If the extracted DNA sequence matches the specified sequence length then the sequence will be translated.
Sequences not containing a stop codon or unknown AA will be saved.

Output files can be used for Filter AA and Filter Motif.
? Experimental substrates.
Acceptable file extensions: .fasta, .fasta.gz, .fa, .fa.gz, .fastq, .fastq.gz, .fq, .fq.gz
? Background substrates.
Acceptable file extensions: .fasta, .fasta.gz, .fa, .fa.gz, .fastq, .fastq.gz, .fq, .fq.gz
? Extract substrate DNA sequence between the 5' and 3' sequences.
Ex: (5') AAAGGCAGT-substrate-GGTGGAAGT (3')
? Minimum quality score of acceptable DNA reads.
? Drop substrates with fewer counts than this value.
? Number of AAs in the substrate.
? Select substrates with a given AA at a defined residue in the sequence.
? Exclude substrates with a given AA at a defined residue in the sequence.
? Remove a position from the sequence.
